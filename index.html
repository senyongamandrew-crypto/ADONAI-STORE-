Adonai Delivery Network

From our store to your door

Every order is packed, confirmed, tracked, and delivered by Adonai Store from our Kampala team to customers across Uganda. Our delivery partners support the route; they are not the whole service.

Central Region

Adonai Same-Day Delivery

Adonai confirms, packs, assigns, and tracks delivery across Kampala (Kololo, Ntinda, Bugolobi, Muyenga, Nakasero), Entebbe & Mukono. Our trusted boda delivery partners handle the route, while our team manages the customer handoff. Free in-store pickup is available at our Kampala store.

Eastern Region

Adonai Regional Courier

Adonai coordinates secure, receipt-verified courier delivery to Jinja, Iganga, Mbale, Tororo, and surrounding districts, with customer updates from dispatch through arrival.

Western Region

Adonai Door-to-Door

Door-to-door delivery coordination to Mbarara, Fort Portal, Masaka, Kabale, Kasese, and other destinations. We package the order carefully, confirm the route, and keep you informed through delivery.
