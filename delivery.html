THE THIRD APP

Storefront. Cashier register. Delivery.

This is the rider's own application — separately installable, separately signed in, and showing only the orders assigned to that rider. It never shares the cashier's session or the shopper's storefront.

    A paid web order is prepared in the Admin Suite and assigned to a delivery partner.

  2. 2
    Rider receives

    The job arrives here with a notification, the customer's area, phone and parcel contents.

  3. 3
    Rider accepts

    Accept, or decline with a reason so dispatch can reassign it immediately.

  4. 4
    Rider delivers

    Collected → arrived → delivered, with the recipient's name recorded at the door.
