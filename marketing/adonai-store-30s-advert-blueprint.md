# Adonai Store — 30-Second Video Advert Blueprint

**Main message:** Shop online at Adonai Store, choose your preferred fits, pay easily, and get fast delivery anywhere in Uganda.

| Item | Specification |
| --- | --- |
| Runtime | 30 seconds (0:00 – 0:30) |
| Primary format | 9:16 vertical, 1080 × 1920, 24 fps (Reels, TikTok, WhatsApp Status, Stories) |
| Secondary format | 16:9 cut-down, 1920 × 1080 (YouTube, website hero), same timing, re-frame captions |
| Language | English voiceover, professional and clear |
| Target audience | Local shoppers in Kampala and across Uganda |
| Tone | Confident, warm, credible, aspirational but attainable. Show real people in real motion, not staged perfection. |
| Key selling points | Easy online ordering · MTN MoMo / Airtel Money / Cash on Delivery · Fast local delivery |

## 3. Production direction

### 3.1 Cast and wardrobe
- **Lead (Talent A):** Ugandan woman or man, 22–32, natural presence. Two wardrobe changes: casual home wear before the delivery, and the featured outfit after unboxing.
- **Rider (Talent B):** Real or experienced boda boda rider. Branded helmet, reflective vest or jacket, phone in a clip. Do not use a rider who is not fit to ride in the shoot conditions.
- **Packing staff (Talent C):** An Adonai team member packing the order at the store counter. Use a real staff member if possible for authenticity.
- **Featured outfit:** Choose one curated 1-of-1 piece and one brand-new piece so both parts of the catalogue appear.

### 3.2 Locations
- **Home or office (Scenes 1 and 5):** A bright Kampala interior or a modern office with natural window light. Avoid clutter. Keep one clothing rack visible in the background.
- **Store / packing area (Scene 4):** The Adonai packing area at store location to be confirmed (not Kibuli), or a clean stand-in space with the Adonai mailer bags.
- **Gate handover (Scene 4):** A residential gate in Kampala, with a rider arriving on a boda. Shoot in the late afternoon for warm light.

### 3.3 Realistic phone-screen content
- Use the **live storefront** or a **staging build with dummy orders**. Do not show real customer names, phone numbers, or order IDs.
- **Browse:** product grid, the Brand-New / Vintage filter chips, and product photos.
- **Product page:** size selection, condition grade (for example "Grade A / Like New"), and the measurements table. This shows the viewer that fit is clear before purchase.
- **Bag and delivery:** the bag drawer with the delivery zone selected (for example Kampala, same or next-day boda).
- **Payment:** the "Pay now: MoMo & Card" and "Pay on delivery / pickup" options. Show the MoMo approval prompt on the phone, with the PIN hidden or masked.
- **Confirmation:** the order confirmation screen with a tick. Keep it on screen for at least 1.5 seconds.

### 3.4 Motion and realism rules
- Every scene has movement: a hand swipes, a rider pulls in, a package changes hands, the subject turns.
- Use handheld or gimbal for the people, and locked-off shots only for establishing frames.
- Use practical sound: rider engine, gate latch, tape, tissue paper, the MoMo ringtone. Record voiceover separately in a quiet room.
- Keep faces readable in every shot. Avoid heavy backlight on the talent during the handover.
- Use one warm colour grade across the film. The brand accent is emerald (`#22C55E`) on deep slate (`#111827`), which can be used in captions and transitions.

## 5. Voiceover

**Direction:** Professional, clear, warm and confident. Keep the pace steady, around 140–150 words per minute. Pause briefly between sentences. Stress the product and the features ("MTN MoMo", "Cash on Delivery", "doorstep"). Keep the read neutral and easy to follow for listeners across Uganda. Do not use slang or a forced accent.

**Final VO lines with cues:**

| Time | Line (exact) | Words | Cue |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00 – 0:06 | "Looking for quality apparel and unique 1-of-1 fits without leaving your home?" | 12 | Rising question tone on "home". |
| 0:06 – 0:12 | "Simply visit adonaithriftstore.com on your phone, pick your favorite items, and place your order in seconds." | 16 | Pace up slightly on "in seconds". |
| 0:12 – 0:18 | "Pay conveniently using MTN MoMo, Airtel Money, or Cash on Delivery." | 11 | Lift on each payment name. |
| 0:18 – 0:24 | "We pack your order carefully and deliver straight to your doorstep—in Kampala and across Uganda." | 16 | Warm and reassuring. Slight pause at the dash. |
| 0:24 – 0:30 | "Adonai Store—Quality fashion delivered to you. Visit adonaithriftstore.com today!" | 10 | Confident close. Pause at the dash, then a clear call to action. |

**Timing note:** Scenes 2 and 4 contain about 16 words each, which is roughly 6.5 seconds at 150 words per minute, in a 6-second slot. Either ask the voice artist to read at about 2.6 words per second, or let the VO run about 0.5 seconds into the next visual. The editor can also remove the one-second breath at the start of the opening visual.

## 6. On-screen captions

**Design rules**
- **Typeface:** a bold geometric sans-serif (for example Montserrat ExtraBold or Inter Black). Use no more than two weights in the whole film.
- **Colour:** white text (`#FFFFFF`) with an emerald (`#22C55E`) highlight on the key word. Place the text on a deep slate (`#111827`) bar at 85% opacity for legibility.
- **Safe zones (9:16):** keep text out of the top 250 px and the bottom 340 px so it is not covered by the platform UI.
- **Animation:** captions slide up over 0.3 seconds and hold for their full scene. No flashing or jitter.
- **Emoji:** kept exactly as in the script. For a more formal finish, remove them from the captions only, and keep them in the social post copy.

## 7. Production checklist

- [ ] Confirm the domain, phone number, and delivery promise (Section 1).
- [ ] Confirm the store is taking orders on the day, and that the staging or dummy orders do not show customer data.
- [ ] Book the rider with a valid licence, helmet and insurance. Pay the rider's standard boda fare.
- [ ] Prepare two outfits, Adonai mailer bags, tissue paper, and order labels.
- [ ] Prepare a test MoMo or Airtel approval flow with a masked PIN.
- [ ] Record voiceover in a quiet room at 48 kHz, WAV, separate from the picture.
- [ ] Shoot a 16:9 and a 9:16 version. Check that captions stay in the safe zones in both.
- [ ] Get written consent from every person on camera, including the rider and staff.
- [ ] Use official MTN and Airtel marks only if permitted.
- [ ] Final QA: spelling of "adonaithriftstore.com", the phone number, and each caption against Section 6.
