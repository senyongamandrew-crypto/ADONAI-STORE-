Legal & Data Protection

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 14, 2026 · Operating under Uganda Data Protection & Privacy Act 2019
Summary for Shoppers: We use your contact and delivery details to confirm, fulfill, and support your order. The storefront also stores limited cart, consent, and browsing-session data in your browser. We do not sell customer details or use them for third-party advertising.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you give us when you browse, place an order, pay, or contact the store. We do not require an email address for web checkout, but you may provide one to staff when needed.

  • Contact details: Name and telephone number for order confirmation and delivery updates; optional email when supplied to the store.
  • Delivery details: Destination area, street or landmark, optional handoff instructions, and notes you submit with an order.
  • Location with permission: If you allow location access at checkout, we use the coordinates to allocate a delivery zone and coordinate your handoff. You can decline permission and choose an area manually.
  • Order and payment records: Items, quantities, price, order and receipt references, selected payment method, and the transaction reference or payer details needed to verify a transfer. We do not collect or ask for your Mobile Money, bank, or card PIN.
  • Browsing and session data: The storefront saves your cart, a temporary reservation/session token, and consent choice in browser storage. A short-lived session identifier and a limited queue of storefront events may also be stored locally; events can include product views, searches, cart actions, and checkout activity.

2. How We Use and Share Your Information

We use contact and order details to reserve stock, confirm payment, prepare receipts, coordinate delivery or pickup, send order updates, and respond to support or return requests. We share only the information needed for the task with authorized Adonai staff, the payment provider you choose, and the assigned rider or courier. These service partners receive details to process or deliver your order, not for their own marketing.

We do not sell your name, phone number, address, payment reference, or browsing history to advertisers. We do not send promotional messages unless you ask to receive them or otherwise agree.

3. Data Protection & Retention

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards and limit access to order and customer records to staff who need them for store operations. We retain transaction and contact records for as long as needed to fulfill orders, resolve disputes, support accounting and fraud prevention, or meet legal obligations. Browser storage remains on your device until you clear it or the stored session expires; clearing browser storage does not erase completed order records held by the store.

4. Cookies, Browsing Sessions & Local Storage

The storefront uses first-party browser storage rather than advertising cookies to remember your cart, temporary reservation state, and privacy choice. A temporary session identifier and a small local event queue help the site understand product views, searches, cart activity, and checkout flow. This storefront does not load a third-party advertising pixel by default. If an analytics provider is enabled in a future deployment, relevant events may be sent to that provider under its privacy terms.
