1. Inventory & Item Grading

Our product labels tell you what to expect before you buy. Inventory availability is live and can change when an order is confirmed.

  • Pre-Loved / Thrift (1-of-1 Pieces): These are hand-inspected, laundered, single-quantity vintage or pre-loved garments. Because there is only one of each piece, reserving one in your cart temporarily holds it during active checkout. The piece is not sold until checkout is completed and the order is confirmed.
  • Brand-New Apparel: These products are sourced directly from manufacturers or brand partners. They can have multi-quantity inventory variants, including different sizes or colours, and are supplied with intact factory tags and original packaging unless the product listing says otherwise.

Item condition: Product photos, descriptions, measurements, and any disclosed flaws form part of the item description. Pre-loved pieces may show ordinary signs of previous wear; we disclose notable defects we identify during inspection. Brand-new items should be in factory-new condition as described on the listing.

2. Pricing & Currency

All item prices shown in the online catalog, in-store tags, and receipts are in Ugandan Shillings (UGX). The item price is separate from any applicable delivery fee, which is confirmed before dispatch. A price is only locked when the order is confirmed; items in a cart or message thread are not a completed purchase.

3. Payment Methods & Verification

Available payment methods are shown at checkout and may include:

  • Cash: Pay at the store counter or by Cash on Delivery / pickup when offered for your order.
  • Card and supported mobile wallets: Secure online checkout through Flutterwave when the gateway is available.
  • Mobile Money: MTN MoMo and Airtel Money by an in-app payment prompt or a published Adonai merchant line.
  • Bank transfer: Available when current bank details are published at checkout.
  • Adonai merchant pay code: A unique order code that can be settled at an Adonai merchant point.

Only choose a payment method that is displayed as available for your order. Do not send a Mobile Money PIN, bank PIN, or one-time password to Adonai Store. We may ask for a transaction reference and the payer details needed to verify payment. An order is confirmed when payment or the agreed Cash on Delivery / pickup arrangement has been verified by our team.

4. Adonai Delivery, Dispatch & COD

Adonai Store manages the complete delivery experience. We confirm the order, prepare and package the items, coordinate the route, share customer updates, and oversee the handoff at the destination. Boda riders and regional couriers are delivery partners who support the last mile; customers are not simply given a parcel and left to coordinate the rest themselves.

  • Kampala and Greater Kampala: Orders confirmed by 4:00 PM are targeted for same-day delivery; orders confirmed later are targeted for the next available delivery day. A boda partner may complete the route while our team manages updates and handoff.
  • Other destinations in Uganda: We target dispatch to the regional courier within one business day after order and payment / COD confirmation. Transit to major towns is typically targeted at 1–3 business days from dispatch. Remote destinations, public holidays, weather, and route availability can take longer; we will confirm the route estimate before dispatch and communicate material delays.
  • In-store pickup: Free collection is available at Plot 45 Salama Road / Kibuli, Kampala. We will contact you when the order is ready and confirm the collection window before you travel.

These are delivery targets, not guaranteed arrival times. The timing starts after the order and its payment or COD arrangement are confirmed. Our team will share the current estimate for your destination before dispatch.

Cash on Delivery confirmation: We require phone or WhatsApp confirmation from the customer before Adonai releases a COD order to its delivery partner. Please keep your phone reachable and have the exact agreed amount ready. If a COD order is rejected at the destination without valid return grounds, the customer remains responsible for the return dispatch fee. This covers the partner’s return trip and is separate from the original delivery fee.

Delays caused by an unreachable recipient, an incorrect address, unsafe access, severe weather, or circumstances outside the delivery partner’s control may require a new dispatch arrangement or additional fee. Our team will communicate the next step.

5. 2-Day Returns & Refunds

For a defective item or one materially graded incorrectly against its listing, contact us within 48 hours (2 days) from receipt. Fit or style preference, change of mind, and flaws disclosed before purchase are not eligible by themselves. Keep the item, tags, packaging, and receipt while we review your request.

Approved returns are inspected and resolved by refund through the original payment method or store credit if you choose credit. Cash / Cash on Delivery purchases are refunded in cash at the agreed handoff; digital payments are returned through their original channel. Contact Customer Care before drop-off or collection: approved items can be handed over at Plot 45 Salama Road / Kibuli, Kampala, or we will confirm a regional courier or pickup plan and any transport fee first.

See the complete 2-Day Return Policy for evidence, condition, refund, store-credit, and handoff instructions.

6. Privacy & Data Protection

Adonai Store handles personal information in accordance with Uganda’s Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019 and applicable regulations. We aim to collect and use only information that is adequate, relevant, and necessary for the service you request.

Purpose limitation: Customer personal details—Name, Contact Number, Mobile Money details, and Delivery Address—are collected solely for order fulfillment, transaction verification, and boda rider dispatches. We will never sell these details or share them with third-party marketers.

  • Mobile Money safety: We do not need, and will never ask for, your Mobile Money PIN. Payment details are limited to the information needed to verify a transaction, such as a transaction reference.
  • Limited operational access: We may disclose the minimum delivery details needed to an assigned boda rider, courier, payment service, or staff member to complete the specific order. Those disclosures are for operations only and are not marketing permissions.
  • Security and retention: We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect order records and retain personal data only for as long as needed for fulfillment, verification, customer support, accounting, fraud prevention, or a legal obligation.
  • Your rights: Subject to the Act and any applicable exceptions, you may ask to access, correct, update, or request deletion of your personal information, or object to a use that is not necessary for your order. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.
  • Concerns: Contact us first using the details below so we can investigate a privacy concern. You may also contact Uganda’s Personal Data Protection Office where you believe your rights under the Act have not been respected.

7. Order Cancellations

To cancel, contact us by phone or WhatsApp as soon as possible and include your order number. Request cancellation before dispatch; we will confirm the order status and explain any payment reversal or refund before closing the order.

  • Before dispatch, an eligible cancellation releases the held stock. A unique pre-loved piece may immediately become available to another shopper.
  • Once an order has been handed to a boda rider or courier, we cannot guarantee it can be stopped. Delivery, return-dispatch, or other already-incurred transport costs may apply and will be explained by our team.
  • For Cash on Delivery, confirm the order and keep your phone reachable. Refusing or not collecting a parcel after dispatch without an approved return reason is a failed delivery; the customer is responsible for the return boda dispatch fee.
  • We may cancel or decline an order if an item is unavailable, payment cannot be verified, an address is unsafe or invalid, or fraud is suspected. We will communicate the reason and any refund or next step.
