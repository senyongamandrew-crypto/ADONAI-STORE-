1. Inventory & Item Grading

Our product labels tell you what to expect before you buy. Inventory availability is live and can change when an order is confirmed.

  • Pre-Loved / Thrift (1-of-1 Pieces): These are hand-inspected, laundered, single-quantity vintage or pre-loved garments. Because there is only one of each piece, reserving one in your cart temporarily holds it during active checkout. The piece is not sold until checkout is completed and the order is confirmed.
  • Brand-New Apparel: These products are sourced directly from manufacturers or brand partners. They can have multi-quantity inventory variants, including different sizes or colours, and are supplied with intact factory tags and original packaging unless the product listing says otherwise.

Item condition: Product photos, descriptions, measurements, and any disclosed flaws form part of the item description. Pre-loved pieces may show ordinary signs of previous wear; we disclose notable defects we identify during inspection. Brand-new items should be in factory-new condition as described on the listing.

2. Pricing & Currency

All item prices shown in the online catalog, in-store tags, and receipts are in Ugandan Shillings (UGX). The item price is separate from any applicable delivery fee, which is confirmed before dispatch. A price is only locked when the order is confirmed; items in a cart or message thread are not a completed purchase.

3. Payment Methods & Verification

We currently accept the following payment methods for eligible orders:

  • Mobile Money: MTN Mobile Money and Airtel Money transfers must be verified against the transaction reference before an order is released.
  • Cash: Cash at the store or Cash on Delivery (COD) for eligible delivery zones and orders.

Do not send a Mobile Money PIN to Adonai Store. We may ask for the transaction reference, sender name, and phone number needed to verify payment. An order is not confirmed until the payment or COD arrangement has been verified by our team.

4. Delivery, Dispatch & COD

  • Kampala and Greater Kampala: Same-day boda delivery may be available for orders confirmed before 4:00 PM. The delivery fee is confirmed at checkout based on the destination and rider availability.
  • Regional Uganda: We use available courier services to dispatch to towns including Jinja, Mbale, Mbarara, Masaka, Gulu, Fort Portal, and surrounding districts. Timing depends on the courier route and destination.
  • In-store pickup: Free collection is available at Plot 45 Salama Road / Kibuli, Kampala, during operating hours: Monday–Saturday, 8:30 AM–8:00 PM; Sunday, 11:00 AM–6:00 PM.

Cash on Delivery confirmation: We require phone or WhatsApp confirmation from the customer before a COD order is handed to a rider for dispatch. Please keep your phone reachable and have the exact agreed amount ready. If a COD order is rejected at the destination without valid return grounds, the customer remains responsible for the return boda dispatch fee. This fee covers the rider’s return trip and is separate from the original delivery fee.

Delays caused by an unreachable recipient, an incorrect address, unsafe access, severe weather, or circumstances outside the rider’s control may require a new dispatch arrangement or additional fee.

5. 2-Day (48-Hour) Return & Exchange Policy

If an item does not fit or differs materially from its description, contact us within 48 hours (2 days) of receipt to request a return or exchange. We will confirm the request and advise on the handover or rider process.

  • Pre-Loved / Thrift: The original attached Adonai Store barcode tag must still be present. The garment must be in the same condition as delivered, unworn, unwashed, and free from new damage, stains, odour, or alterations.
  • Brand-New Apparel: The item must be unworn and returned with all original factory tags attached and the inner packaging intact. Items missing factory tags or with damaged, opened, or incomplete inner packaging may not qualify for a return or exchange.

Returns are not accepted for change of mind, misuse, wear after delivery, or failure to follow the care instructions. Where an exchange is unavailable, any refund or store credit will be assessed against the verified condition and the payment method used.

6. Privacy & Data Protection

Adonai Store handles personal information in accordance with Uganda’s Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019 and applicable regulations. We aim to collect and use only information that is adequate, relevant, and necessary for the service you request.

Purpose limitation: Customer personal details—Name, Contact Number, Mobile Money details, and Delivery Address—are collected solely for order fulfillment, transaction verification, and boda rider dispatches. We will never sell these details or share them with third-party marketers.

  • Mobile Money safety: We do not need, and will never ask for, your Mobile Money PIN. Payment details are limited to the information needed to verify a transaction, such as a transaction reference.
  • Limited operational access: We may disclose the minimum delivery details needed to an assigned boda rider, courier, payment service, or staff member to complete the specific order. Those disclosures are for operations only and are not marketing permissions.
  • Security and retention: We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect order records and retain personal data only for as long as needed for fulfillment, verification, customer support, accounting, fraud prevention, or a legal obligation.
  • Your rights: Subject to the Act and any applicable exceptions, you may ask to access, correct, update, or request deletion of your personal information, or object to a use that is not necessary for your order. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.
  • Concerns: Contact us first using the details below so we can investigate a privacy concern. You may also contact Uganda’s Personal Data Protection Office where you believe your rights under the Act have not been respected.

7. Order Cancellations

For a cancellation request, contact us as soon as possible by phone or WhatsApp and include your order details. Before dispatch, we will confirm whether cancellation is possible and whether a payment reversal, refund, or store credit applies.

  • A pre-loved 1-of-1 item may become available to another shopper once your temporary cart hold or order is cancelled.
  • Orders already handed to a boda rider or courier may not be cancellable without delivery or return costs being incurred.
  • For COD, failing to confirm, being unreachable, or rejecting the parcel at the destination without valid return grounds is treated as a failed delivery. The customer remains responsible for the return boda dispatch fee.
  • We may cancel or decline an order if an item is unavailable, a payment cannot be verified, a delivery address is unsafe or invalid, or we suspect fraud. We will communicate the reason and the available resolution.
