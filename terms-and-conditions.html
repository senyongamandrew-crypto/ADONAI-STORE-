Store Policies & Agreement

Terms & Conditions

Last Updated: September 30, 2026 · Governing Law: Republic of Uganda
Key Shopping Notice: All apparel items at Adonai Thrift Store are genuine curated pre-loved vintage pieces (1-of-1). Each garment is hand-inspected, laundered, photographed in daylight, and sold once. Once checked out, pieces are marked out-of-stock across all channels.

1. Inventory & 1-of-1 Piece Reservations

Because garments are unique single pieces, placing an item in your bag reserves it temporarily during your checkout session. To guarantee claim of an item, complete your order confirmation via WhatsApp or at our in-store POS register.

2. Pricing & Currency

All prices listed across the online catalog, in-store tags, and printed receipts are in Ugandan Shillings (UGX). Prices displayed are final and inclusive of standard item grading.

3. Payment Methods & Verification

We accept the following payment options:

  • Cash Tender: In-store at our checkout counter or Cash on Delivery (COD) for eligible central Kampala boda deliveries.
  • MTN Mobile Money: Verified merchant payment transfer with required transaction reference ID.
  • Airtel Money: Verified mobile wallet payment with transaction confirmation.

4. Delivery & Dispatch Guidelines

  • Central Kampala & Greater Kampala: Same-day boda delivery for orders confirmed before 4:00 PM. Standard boda delivery fee is UGX 7,000 (calculated at checkout based on neighborhood).
  • Regional Uganda (Eastern & Western): Next-day courier dispatch to Jinja, Mbale, Mbarara, Masaka, Gulu, Fort Portal, and surrounding districts.
  • In-Store Pickup: Free collection available at our store (Plot 45 Salama Road / Kibuli, Kampala) during operating hours (Mon–Sat 8:30 AM – 8:00 PM, Sun 11:00 AM – 6:00 PM).

5. 2-Day Return & Exchange Policy

We take pride in transparent grading. If a piece does not fit or differs significantly from its described condition, you may exchange it within 48 hours (2 days) of receipt, provided the original barcode tag remains attached and the garment is in its delivered condition.
