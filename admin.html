DELIVERY ACCOUNTABILITY

Latest delivery updates

    ANDROID SCREEN-LOCK TRACKING

    Pair → start duty → deliver

    1. Add a delivery partner and create a private pairing code.
    2. The rider pairs their phone in the updated Android app and explicitly starts GPS sharing.
    3. Assign a prepared, paid delivery order. The rider reports acceptance, collection, arrival and recipient-confirmed delivery.
    4. End sharing from the app or its ongoing notification. Each duty ends after 12 hours.

    GPS, mobile data and notifications must be enabled. Battery controls, force-stop or lost network can interrupt updates; the locator shows stale data honestly. Existing APKs need a rebuild/install for this service.
